What changes mean

This follows the snapshot-and-movie approach in the supplied dynamic.R: ordered time slices, persistent node identities, and a movie revealing changing ties. Here each slice is a calendar month based on transaction date. Multiple report rows for the same filer and ticker become one undirected activity edge; line width reflects the number of rows, not transaction value.

Green solid edges are present now but absent in the preceding calendar month. Gray edges occur in both. Orange dashed edges occurred in the preceding month but are absent now; they fade away during the transition. January has no earlier comparison, so its edges are labeled initial. Filters apply consistently to both months.

Based only on the validated 2025 analytical CSV (6,394 House and 518 Senate report rows), separate from the broader completion table. A missing edge means no matching row in this captured monthly sample. It does not establish a sale, an ended holding, or an absence of trading. Filers can report household activity and amendments. Shared tickers do not establish personal relationships or coordination. Coverage is incomplete and differs by chamber.
